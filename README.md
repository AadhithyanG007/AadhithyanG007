## About Me

I'm a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student at the Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), Angamaly, graduating in 2027, and currently serving as my institution's Placement Cell Coordinator. My pattern is consistent: I take what I learn — from coursework, internships, or research — and rebuild it into something more complete than what was originally asked for.

That habit is what produced **RuneCore**, an NPA (Non-Performing Asset) lifecycle management suite I built independently after my internship at Safenet (Palakkad), where I worked on a Canara Bank recovery platform in ASP.NET MVC and Oracle. Instead of leaving that domain knowledge in an internship report, I extended it into a standalone production-grade system with row-level security, an immutable audit ledger, and real concurrency handling. At Safenet, I'm also currently contributing to **DGymBook**, a multi-tenant gym management SaaS the company is building — I've worked on its UI/UX and helped with early system design, while the platform is still in its initial development phase. Alongside that, **SKEIN**, my final-year capstone, applies Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search to dynamic vehicle routing.

Beyond building, I care about understanding *why* something works. I co-authored a peer-reviewed paper on sentiment analysis of vibe-coding tools (published in IJCA, 2025), comparing four ML classifiers across 4,000+ reviews. I'm drawn to systems-level problems — database concurrency, distributed architecture, applied optimization, and AI systems that need deterministic guardrails rather than unchecked automation — all shaped by direct exposure to a regulated, compliance-heavy environment during my internship.

I work in a direct, iterative way: I'd rather ship a targeted, well-reasoned fix than rewrite something that's already 90% right, and I like being handed clear constraints and trusted to make the structural calls myself.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏦 RuneCore — Canara Recovery Enterprise Suite
**Production-grade NPA (Non-Performing Asset) lifecycle management platform**, independently built by extending domain knowledge from a bank-recovery internship into a standalone system.

`React 19` `TanStack Router` `Node.js` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Zustand` `Docker`

- Full SARFAESI legal recovery workflow modeled as an explicit stage machine, plus OTS (One-Time Settlement) negotiation and multi-stage approval chains
- Row-Level Security + BRIN indexing for efficient, tenant-safe querying at scale
- `SELECT FOR UPDATE` concurrency control to safely handle simultaneous case updates
- Immutable audit ledger implemented via database trigger rather than application logic, so it can't be bypassed
- Dual-token JWT auth with Argon2id password hashing and account lockout
- A CBS (Core Banking System) simulator microservice plus 5 scheduled cron jobs mirroring real bank data flows and compliance tasks
- **Problem it solves:** demonstrates how banks manage distressed assets end-to-end, from CBS ingestion through legal recovery to final settlement, in one cohesive system

## 🧭 Coding Philosophy

> If it doesn't feel production-ready, it isn't finished.

I'd rather ship a targeted, well-reasoned fix — or three fewer features, fully load-tested and documented — than rewrite something that's already 90% right or ship everything half-done. I default to understanding *why* a system is built the way it is before I touch it, explicit code over clever code, and clear constraints over vague direction — that's where good engineering decisions actually get made. Architecture should hold up under real load, not just demo well. I treat resumes, READMEs, and demos with the same standard I treat the underlying system: if it's not accurate and it's not clean, it's not done.
